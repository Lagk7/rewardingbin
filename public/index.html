How Eco Rewards Work

Our platform rewards community members for diverting waste from landfills into recycling streams. Every 10 grams of recyclable material earns you 1 Eco Point.

⚖️ The 10g = 1 Point Formula

Points are calculated simply and transparently:

• 80 grams (e.g. plastic bottle) = 8 Points
• 150 grams (e.g. soda can) = 15 Points
• 350 grams (e.g. glass bottle) = 35 Points
• 1,000 grams (1 kg) = 100 Points

🏅 Eco Champion Tiers

Climb the tiers as your lifetime points increase:

• 🥉 Bronze Recycler: 0 – 29 Points
• 🥈 Silver Recycler: 30 – 79 Points
• 🥇 Gold Eco Champion: 80 – 149 Points
• 🏆 Platinum Planet Saver: 150+ Points
